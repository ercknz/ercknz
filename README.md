## Hi, I'm Erick H Nuñez, PhD 👋

**Biomedical engineer** working in rehabilitation robotics, mechatronics, and engineering lab operations.
I build robotic and wearable systems for people recovering from stroke, from the CAD model and machined parts to the control code and the human-subject study.

- 🎓 PhD in Biomedical Engineering, NJIT (2026): *Towards a Data-Driven and Mechatronics Framework for Post-Stroke Motor Rehabilitation*
- 🔧 Director of Laboratories, NJIT Biomedical Engineering: 3D printing studio, machine shop, and teaching labs; ~18 capstone teams a year
- 🤖 Interested in robot control, exoskeletons, wearable sensing, and digital twins
- 🎲 Fun fact: I love board games, martial arts, and Star Trek
